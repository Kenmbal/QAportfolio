Workflow automationPortfolio project

Veterinary Vaccine Reminder System

An n8n workflow concept for helping a veterinary clinic track pet vaccine due dates and send timely reminders.

Workflow
  • Reads pet records from Google Sheets and validates required data.
  • Calculates reminder dates and sends emails 30 days before, 7 days before, on the due date, and 7 days overdue.
  • Logs invalid records and send activity, with Slack alerts for workflow failures.
  • Uses fake data and a test mode that routes emails to a single inbox.
Design focus

Scheduled processing, data validation, traceable logs, controlled testing, and failure notifications.
